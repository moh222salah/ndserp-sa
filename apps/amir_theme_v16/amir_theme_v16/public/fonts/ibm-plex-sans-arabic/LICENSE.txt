Files here are the ARABIC SUBSET only (unicode-range U+0600-06FF and the
Arabic presentation-form blocks), extracted from the Google Fonts build.
Latin glyphs are deliberately excluded — Inter, already shipped by Frappe,
renders Latin, so shipping Latin twice would waste bytes.

Weights bundled: 400, 500, 600, 700.
Weight 300 is not bundled: unused by this theme.
